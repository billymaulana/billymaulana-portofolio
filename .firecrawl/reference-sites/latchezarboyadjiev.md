Meet the Artist

## The Art of Shaping Glass

_“I was always drawn to form — the way it breathes, the way it holds space. Classical sculpture gave me discipline, but it was glass that gave me freedom. In its fluidity and light, I found a voice that no other material could offer.”_

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/680f2ba2ed84d094c8510e0b/681b1ec78000ae3650ec3406_Djamina%20Red%20Logo.avif)

_“Working with glass is like shaping motion itself. It’s alive, transparent, and constantly shifting. In every piece, I try to capture that quiet energy — the moment between stillness and becoming.”_

![Artist Latchezar Boyadjiev carving or sketching in studio](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/680f2ba2ed84d094c8510e0b/6823526a22a4ec6671b468ae_image.avif)
