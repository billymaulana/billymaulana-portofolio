This typeface carries just the right amount of flair to double as a powerful display option. Whether used in body copy or in bold, eye-catching headlines, it commands attention with effortless grace.

Perfectly suited for designs aiming to capture that nostalgic, mid-90s editorial aesthetic, Editorial New blends retro charm with a distinctly modern edge. It feels both familiar and fresh—like a vintage magazine reimagined for today’s digital canvas. In its lighter weights, it exudes a quiet sophistication: refined, subtle, and dripping with elegance. It's easy to see it gracing the pages of high-end fashion magazines or lending an air of luxury to brand identities in the beauty or design industries. But don’t be fooled—this typeface isn't just about being soft-spoken. The regular weight is a workhorse for immersive reading experiences, offering clean legibility and a steady rhythm, ideal for thoughtful editorial layouts. And when the mood shifts to bold, the heavier weights rise to the occasion. With fuller curves and heightened contrast, they become expressive and striking—just the thing for making strong typographic statements without shouting. Then there’s the italics: luscious, curvaceous, and undeniably stylish. They add a fluid, expressive dimension that elevates everything from pull quotes to callouts, infusing your design with warmth and character. Editorial New is a typographic chameleon: refined yet charismatic, versatile yet opinionated. It’s a must-try for any designer looking to strike the perfect balance between classic editorial sophistication and contemporary style.

## Elegant, refined and subtle.

A precise and elegant narrow serif that was designed for the long-form copy with a big enough personality to make a statement as a title. It is perfect to give your design that mid 90s retro feel while being rich and contemporary.

In its lighter weights, Editorial New is elegant, refined and subtle. It’s easy to imagine it being used for the fashion industry in brands or magazines. With its precise legibility, the regular weight is perfect for that long-form editorial content but it is far from being blend. It will give your text a unique feel and style.

The heavier weights are made for big statements. The curves are lushier, a bit more exaggerated to give these weights and ultimately your designs more personality. Combined with the luscious and curvy italics, this font is a must try on every design.
