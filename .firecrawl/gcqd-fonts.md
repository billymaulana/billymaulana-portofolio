## End of an era

[Permalink: End of an era](https://github.com/g-cqd/Fonts#end-of-an-era)

As the regulars here might have noticed, the repository is now empty.

Just as Netflix did in 2020, [Monotype](https://www.monotype.com/) and [Dinamo](https://www.abcdinamo.com/) recently came across the repository and (legitimately) asked me to remove their typefaces. They reached out kindly, and from their words, I understand that other foundries would likely follow soon as well.

Since it was never my goal to fight against them — but rather to help circumvent paywalls — I fully comply with their requests and have removed all the material from here. The curated list of fonts, foundries, and designers remains below, because they are all very talented, and I believe it can still help guide you toward those great resources.

This repository was a big part of my journey as a designer, and I appreciate all the attention it received from students, enthusiasts, and random people across the Internet.

**Please, remember check copyrights, licensing options and usage conditions of any typeface before using it.**
