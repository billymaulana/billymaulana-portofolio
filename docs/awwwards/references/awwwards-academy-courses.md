### Core Principles (disarikan dari 11 courses)

**1. Strategy Before Pixels**
Muncul di 6 dari 11 courses. Setiap instructor menekankan bahwa design tanpa strategy hanya dekorasi. Pipeline universal: Brief > Research > Strategy > Moodboard > References > Concept > Design. Helene Vetik (Nordic): "brand values should drive design decisions." Media.Monks: "business goals, brand identity, users, dan culture harus inform setiap keputusan."

**2. Emotional Resonance as The Goal**
Bukan visual polish, tapi FEELING yang ditimbulkan. Britton Stipetic menyederhanakan ke formula: **Emotion + Culture + Aesthetics = Unforgettable Brand**. Olga Shevchenko: "emotional web design -- designing to attract audiences emotionally." Duarte Pires: "the best art direction makes you FEEL something."

**3. Typography IS The Design**
Mario Sestak: "Type communicates before it is read." Award winners konsisten menggunakan display typography di extreme sizes (clamp hingga 13rem). Rules: kuasai aturan dulu, baru break dengan intention. Pairing typeface adalah skill kritis -- salah pilih langsung menghancurkan premium perception. Technical precision (kerning, leading) di display sizes itu non-negotiable.

**4. Art Direction as Umbrella Skill**
Duarte Pires: art direction governs semua design decision lain. Ini bukan "look and feel" tapi coherent vision yang menyatukan typography, color, motion, interaction, dan content. Art direction harus ditetapkan SEBELUM visual design dimulai.

**5. Custom Grids as Differentiator**
Standard 12-column grid menghasilkan standard-looking sites. Olga Shevchenko dan Viacheslav Olianishyn: custom grid systems yang intentionally break conventions. Grid bukan hanya alignment tool tapi perceptual tool yang mengurangi cognitive load (Gestalt principles).

**6. Train Your Eye Deliberately**
Viacheslav Olianishyn memperkenalkan "Well-Watched Skill" -- deliberate practice menganalisis visual work untuk melatih design taste. Marek Suchanek: "be your own curator" dan deconstruct award-winning projects secara sistematis. Taste is trainable, bukan bawaan lahir.

**7. The "Wholesome Designer" Advantage**
Marek Suchanek: designer yang menang awards di 2026 itu multi-disciplinary. Pure UI specialist kalah dari yang juga menguasai illustration, photography, custom type, video, dan motion. Semakin banyak skill yang converge, semakin distinctive output-nya.

### Core Principles (dari 5 courses)

**1. Purpose-Driven Animation (bukan dekorasi)**
Louis Paquet mengklasifikasikan SEMUA animation ke 3 purpose: Guide Attention, Create Emotion, atau Provide Feedback. Jika animasi tidak serve salah satu, HAPUS. Louis Ansa menambahkan: "not just grids and margins, but timing and emotions."

**2. Animation as System (bukan ad-hoc effects)**
Louis Ansa: treat animation tokens (timing, easing, duration) dengan rigor yang sama seperti color tokens dan spacing tokens. Build reusable animated master components. Animation system = design system untuk motion.

**3. Three-Act Narrative Structure**
Chiara Aliotta: struktur halaman web = three-act story. Act I: Hook/Hero (beginning). Act II: Development/features/proof (middle). Act III: Resolution/CTA (ending). Setiap section harus evoke planned emotional response.

**4. Timing, Easing, Offset = Craft Quality**
Perbedaan antara skor 4.0 dan 4.5 di Awwwards sering ada di timing. Guideline ranges: micro-interactions (150-300ms), transitions (300-500ms), page reveals (500-1000ms). Stagger/offset creates rhythm -- sequential entry terasa lebih crafted.

**5. Exaggeration > Subtlety untuk Awards**
Marcel Hita Baro & Jaume Mestre: push timing dan scale beyond realism. Subtle itu forgettable, bold itu memorable. Tapi bold harus tetap purposeful.

### Animation System Framework (Louis Ansa)

```
Animation Token System
├── Duration Tokens
│   ├── --duration-micro: 150ms    (button feedback, toggles)
│   ├── --duration-fast: 300ms     (hover states, tooltips)
│   ├── --duration-normal: 500ms   (panel transitions, reveals)
│   ├── --duration-slow: 800ms     (page sections, scroll reveals)
│   └── --duration-slower: 1200ms  (page transitions, hero sequences)
│
├── Easing Tokens
│   ├── --ease-out-expo            (dramatic exit/enter)
│   ├── --ease-out-quart           (smooth natural stop)
│   ├── --ease-in-out              (symmetrical motion)
│   └── --ease-elastic             (playful bounce, sparing use)
│
├── Stagger Patterns
│   ├── Sequential: 50-100ms gap   (list items, nav links)
│   ├── Wave: varied delay          (grid items, particles)
│   └── Cascading: parent > child   (container first, content follows)
│
└── Animated Master Components
    ├── Page enter/exit transitions
    ├── Scroll-triggered reveals (fadeInUp, revealText, parallax)
    ├── Hover state animations
    ├── Loading/preloader sequences
    └── Micro-interaction feedback
```

Prinsip: setiap animated component harus bisa di-reuse, punya lifecycle (init > show > hide > destroy), dan di-document sebagai spec untuk developer.

### Purpose-Driven Motion (Louis Paquet -- 3 Purpose Classification)

| Purpose | When to Use | Examples | Duration Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Guide Attention** | User perlu tahu ke mana harus fokus | Scroll-triggered reveals, highlight pulses, pointer follows | 200-500ms |
| **Create Emotion** | Membangun feeling/brand personality | Hero entrance sequences, parallax depth, fluid distortions | 500-1500ms |
| **Provide Feedback** | User needs confirmation of action | Button clicks, form submissions, loading states, success/error | 100-300ms |

Rule: jika animasi tidak masuk ke salah satu kategori, itu dekorasi -- evaluasi apakah layak dipertahankan.

Dari background di Locomotive: **smooth scroll + well-timed scroll-triggered animations = award-winning baseline**. Ini minimum entry point, bukan differentiator.

### Core Principles (dari 4 courses)

**1. Custom > Off-the-Shelf (selalu)**
Bizarro membangun SELURUH website tanpa framework -- vanilla JS + ES6 classes + custom component system. Bruno Imbrizi membuat generative visuals dari scratch. Celia Lopez hand-crafts setiap 3D asset. Pattern: award winners tidak pakai template atau stock. Mereka build.

**2. Performance is Non-Negotiable**
Celia Lopez: power-of-two textures, polygon budgeting (10K-50K mobile, 100K-500K desktop). Bizarro: OGL over Three.js karena lighter bundle ketika hanya perlu image distortion. Bruno Imbrizi: efficient particle systems yang tidak kill framerate. 60fps atau bust.

**3. The Full Pipeline Matters**
Setiap course mengajarkan complete pipeline, bukan isolated tricks. Concept > Model > Texture > Optimize > Export > Integrate > Deploy. Memahami full workflow memisahkan profesional dari hobbyist.

**4. Interaction Drives Engagement**
Mouse-reactive particles (Imbrizi), hover-triggered GLSL distortions (Bizarro), smooth page transitions (Bizarro), audio-reactive visuals (Imbrizi). "Wow factor" datang dari responsiveness terhadap user input.

**5. Storytelling First, Technology Second**
Semua course menekankan bahwa visual effect atau technology harus serve narrative purpose. Tech yang impressive tapi tanpa konteks = gimmick.

### Floema Architecture (Bizarro)

Blueprint arsitektur website award-winning tanpa framework (applicable sebagai reference pattern meskipun kita pakai Nuxt):

```
Floema Architecture (Vanilla JS, SOTD Winner)
├── Server: Express + Prismic CMS
│   └── SSR untuk SEO + initial load
│
├── Client Architecture
│   ├── App (singleton)
│   │   ├── Component Registry
│   │   ├── Page Router (Fetch API + History API)
│   │   └── Lifecycle Manager
│   │
│   ├── Pages (ES6 classes)
│   │   ├── create()   → DOM creation
│   │   ├── show()     → animate-in (GSAP timeline)
│   │   ├── hide()     → animate-out (GSAP timeline)
│   │   └── destroy()  → cleanup, remove listeners
│   │
│   ├── Components (reusable ES6 classes)
│   │   ├── Preloader
│   │   ├── Navigation
│   │   ├── Canvas (WebGL via OGL)
│   │   └── Smooth Scroll
│   │
│   └── WebGL Layer (OGL)
│       ├── Single canvas, full viewport
│       ├── GLSL shaders untuk image distortion
│       ├── Mouse interaction → shader uniforms
│       └── Resize handling
│
├── Page Transition Flow
│   ├── 1. User clicks link
│   ├── 2. Current page.hide() → Promise (animate out)
│   ├── 3. Fetch new page content
│   ├── 4. Swap DOM content
│   ├── 5. New page.create()
│   ├── 6. New page.show() → Promise (animate in)
│   └── 7. Update History API

├── Libraries
│   ├── GSAP (animation orchestration)
```

**Mapping ke Nuxt stack kita**: Nuxt handles routing/SSR/build, tapi prinsip page transition (animate-out > swap > animate-in), WebGL layer (single canvas + shader uniforms), dan component lifecycle (init/start/stop/destroy) sudah adopted di composables kita.

### 3D-to-WebGL Pipeline (Celia Lopez)

#### The Four Rules (non-negotiable)

| Rule | Detail | Impact jika dilanggar |
|---|---|---|
| **Triangles or Rectangles** | Pastikan topology clean -- tris atau quads, bukan n-gons | Rendering artifacts, broken geometry di WebGL |
| **Power-of-Two Textures** | Dimensions: 256, 512, 1024, 2048 px | GPU tidak bisa mipmap properly, visual quality drop + memory waste |
| **Center Your Objects** | Proper origin points, scene organization | Transform issues, rotation pivot salah, scaling artifacts |
| **Check Your Normals** | Face normals harus consistent direction | Black faces, inside-out rendering, lighting bugs |

#### Complete Pipeline

### Recurring Patterns (muncul di 3+ courses)

**Pattern 1: "Emotion First" (9+ courses)**
Hampir setiap instructor menyebut emotional connection sebagai pembeda utama. Bukan visual complexity, bukan technical prowess -- tapi apakah visitor MERASAKAN sesuatu. Implikasi: setiap design/animation decision harus dijawab dengan "what does this make the visitor FEEL?"

**Pattern 2: "Strategy > Aesthetics" (6+ courses)**
Research dan strategy mendahului visual work. Brief extraction, moodboarding, reference analysis, brand positioning -- semua terjadi SEBELUM satu pixel pun dibuat. Ini berlaku bahkan untuk personal portfolio.

**Pattern 3: "Systematic Approach" (6+ courses)**
Design systems, animation token systems, component-based architecture, reusable patterns. Award-winning sites terasa cohesive karena built on systems, bukan sekumpulan individual decisions.

**Pattern 4: "Typography as Hero Element" (5+ courses)**
Display sizes yang extreme, intentional rule-breaking, type as THE design element. Typography bukan secondary -- di banyak SOTD winners, typography IS the design.

**Pattern 5: "Motion as Meaning" (5+ courses)**
Setiap animasi harus serve purpose (guide, emote, feedback). Animation system = design system untuk motion. Timing/easing/stagger quality adalah pembeda craft level.

**Pattern 6: "Custom Everything" (4+ courses)**
Custom grids, custom animations, custom 3D assets, custom code architecture. Off-the-shelf = ordinary. Award winners build their own tools.

**Pattern 7: "Complete Pipeline Mastery" (4+ courses)**
Memahami full workflow dari concept ke deploy. Specialists yang hanya tahu satu layer tidak bisa create cohesive experiences. "Wholesome designer" advantage.

### Immediate Action Items for Portfolio

Diprioritaskan berdasarkan impact-to-effort ratio dan current project state:

**Tier 1 -- High Impact, Existing Infrastructure (minggu ini)**

1. **Audit animation purpose** -- Review setiap GSAP timeline di `useScrollAnimations.ts`: apakah guide, emote, atau feedback? Hapus yang tidak serve purpose. (Paquet)

2. **Verify three-act narrative structure** -- Map `index.vue` section flow ke: Hook (Hero) > Development (About, Projects, Manifesto) > Resolution (Contact, Footer). Pastikan emotional arc naik. (Aliotta)

3. **Refine stagger/offset patterns** -- Di scroll reveals, gunakan sequential stagger (50-100ms gap) bukan all-at-once. Perbedaan kecil tapi visible. (Ansa, Paquet)

4. **Typography audit at extreme sizes** -- Test hero typography di viewport terbesar. Cek kerning, leading, optical alignment. Flaws magnified at 100px+. (Sestak)

**Tier 2 -- Medium Impact, Some Development Needed (2 minggu)**

5. **Document animation token system** -- Formalize `--duration-*` dan `--ease-*` tokens di `main.css`. Ensure ALL animations reference tokens, bukan hardcoded values. (Ansa)

6. **Emotion-per-section mapping** -- Assign target emotion ke setiap section: Hero=awe, About=connection, Projects=respect/trust, Manifesto=depth, Contact=action. Verify design supports each. (Aliotta, Stipetic)

7. **Enhance page transition quality** -- Pastikan ada animate-out > transition state > animate-in sequence. Gunakan Promise-based flow pattern dari Bizarro. (Bizarro)

8. **Add randomness on reload** -- Seed-based variation di `useFluidSimulation` atau hero visuals sehingga setiap kunjungan slightly different. (Imbrizi, Suchanek)

**Tier 3 -- High Impact, Significant Development (1 bulan)**

9. **Lottie-based preloader** -- Replace simple preloader dengan crafted Lottie animation sequence. Ini first impression moment. (Paquet)

10. **3D glass prism object** -- Model + texture glass/prism hero object mengikuti Celia Lopez pipeline (Four Rules, glTF export). Load via OGL di hero section. (Lopez)

11. **Optional sound design** -- Subtle ambient audio dengan user consent toggle. Web Audio API frequency data driving visual params. (Mestre, Imbrizi)

12. **Styleframe key moments** -- Design 3-5 key scroll moments sebagai static compositions di Figma SEBELUM coding. Validate visual impact before development. (Hita Baro)

**Tier 4 -- Future Differentiator**

13. **WebAR experience** -- AR business card atau "view portfolio in your space" via 8th Wall. Masih sangat rare. (AR course)

14. **Case study mini-films** -- Struktur portfolio cases sebagai narrative: structure, music, direction. (Shevchenko)
